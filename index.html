Char Dham Yatra & Pilgrimage Tour Packages from Haridwar

Haridwar is widely recognized as the sacred gateway to the holy Chota Char Dham Yatra. Our agency offers comprehensive 10 to 12-day Char Dham Yatra tour packages starting directly from Haridwar or Rishikesh, covering all four holy shrines: Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, and Badrinath.

For pilgrims with limited time, we organize 5 to 7-day Do Dham Yatra packages focusing on Kedarnath and Badrinath. We also provide specialized 4-day Kedarnath trek packages, complete with hotel accommodations, local transport, priority Yatra registration guidance, and optional helicopter ticket booking assistance for a hassle-free spiritual journey.

Haridwar Taxi Services, Airport Transfers & Vehicle Rentals

In addition to organized holiday packages, Smart Choice Tour and Travels operates a 24/7 taxi rental service in Haridwar. Our diverse vehicle fleet features clean, air-conditioned sedans such as the Swift Dzire for budget travelers, spacious SUVs like the Maruti Ertiga and Toyota Innova Crysta for family trips, and 12 to 17-seater Tempo Travellers for larger travel groups.

Popular Uttarakhand Tourist Destinations & Customized Itineraries

Beyond pilgrimage tours, Uttarakhand boasts breathtaking hill stations and adventure destinations. We design custom tour packages for Mussoorie (Queen of the Hills), featuring visits to Kempty Falls, Gun Hill, and Mall Road.
